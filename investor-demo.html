Investment Thesis

Why veteran housing now

Three structural advantages converge to create a rare impact-return opportunity that institutional capital has overlooked.

🏛️
Government-Backed Revenue
HUD-VASH and SSVF vouchers guarantee rental income directly from the VA and HUD — the most creditworthy tenants in America. Zero collection risk. Zero eviction exposure.
📈
Structural Supply Gap
580,000+ veterans experience housing instability annually. Existing supply meets less than 30% of demand. Every unit placed generates immediate, measurable impact and guaranteed occupancy.
🎖️
Mission-Aligned Returns
ESG mandates and CRA requirements are driving institutional capital toward veteran housing. First movers establish track records before the market becomes crowded. The window is now.
⚖️
Regulatory Moat
VCG holds established relationships with VA medical centers, HUD regional offices, and veteran service organizations — relationships that take years to build and cannot be replicated quickly.
🔒
Downside Protection
VA lease guarantees provide a hard floor on returns. Properties underwritten conservatively at 80% occupancy regularly achieve 95%+. Downside is structurally limited by federal backing.
🤖
Technology Edge
VetConnect OS — our proprietary AI-powered operating platform — monitors every veteran, voucher, and payment in real time. Institutional-grade management at a fraction of traditional overhead.
